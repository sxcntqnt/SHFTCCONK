Friday afternoon on the Rongai route is where the week either ends well or collapses entirely.

This report is for professionals who want to protect their Friday evenings — not arrive home at 9pm physically drained and emotionally done.

## What actually happened last Friday

**3:30pm departure from CBD:** Commuters reported smooth movement to Bomas. Journey to Rongai: 55 minutes. Good.

**4:30pm departure from CBD:** The Langata Road–Bomas junction was congested. Movement slowed significantly at Karen Hardy. Journeys stretched to 90 minutes.

**5:30pm – 7:00pm:** This is the zone. Everyone who left in this window reported 2–2.5 hour journeys. Several commuters reported being stuck between Bomas and the Rongai turn-off for over 45 minutes.

**After 8:00pm:** Road clears significantly. Journey drops back to 60 minutes.

## The professional approach to Friday commuting

Stress management on Fridays is not about calming yourself in traffic. It is about not being in traffic when you do not need to be.

**Option 1: Leave early.** If your office allows it, a 3:00pm departure on Friday gets you home before the compression starts. Use the afternoon hour at a café near your office to wrap up your week productively.

**Option 2: Leave late.** Stay at the office until 7:30pm — use that time for deep work that requires quiet. Leave when the road clears. Home by 9pm, but calm.

**Option 3: Plan around it.** Use the Matatu Pulse community reports every Thursday evening to know what Friday typically looks like on your specific route. Then make a decision — not a reaction.

The commuters who dread Fridays are the ones who leave at 5:30pm and spend two hours in a vehicle, arriving home already defeated. The ones who thrive are the ones who planned the exit before the day started.
