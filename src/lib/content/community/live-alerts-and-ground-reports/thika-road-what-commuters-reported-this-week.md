This is a weekly summary of what Matatu Pulse community members have reported along the Thika Road corridor. If you commute from Kasarani, Roysambu, Githurai, Garden City, or Muthaiga, this is your ground-level intelligence briefing.

## What was reported this week

**Monday morning (6:30am – 8:00am)**
Multiple reports of slow movement between Allsops and Pangani due to a broken-down lorry in the left lane. Commuters who left before 6:30am were unaffected. Those who left at 7:00am reported 90+ minute journeys.

**Tuesday and Wednesday**
Clear movement across both days. Fastest reported journey: 38 minutes, Kasarani to Archives, departing 6:45am.

**Thursday**
Roadworks reported near Muthaiga roundabout from 7am. Right lane partially blocked. Vehicles diverting via Limuru Road added 15–20 minutes for commuters coming from Garden City direction.

**Friday**
Classic Friday compression. Volume builds earlier — by 7am the road was already at Thursday-peak levels. Community recommendation: leave before 6:30am or after 9:30am on Fridays.

## What this means for your planning

The stress management value here is not in reading dramatic commute stories. It is in using patterns to make calm, informed decisions the night before.

**The commuters who had good weeks were not lucky. They checked reports the evening before and adjusted their departure by 20–30 minutes.**

If you left at 6:45am on Monday, you sailed through. If you left at 7:15am, you were sitting in traffic. Same road. 30-minute difference.
