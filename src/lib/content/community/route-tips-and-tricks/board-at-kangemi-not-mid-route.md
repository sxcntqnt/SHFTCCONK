If you board Route 46 mid-route, you're fighting for a seat in a vehicle that's already half full and paying a fare set by however squeezed the driver feels.

Board at **Kangemi terminus** instead:

- You get first pick of seats
- Fare is set at the official terminus rate
- The vehicle usually leaves faster since the sacco wants to keep turnover high

**Net result:** ~20 minutes saved and KES 20 cheaper, most days.
