Every Tuesday I have a standing 8am client meeting in Upper Hill. I leave Umoja at 6:30am and I still sometimes arrive at 7:58am, walking in sweaty and flustered.

I know there must be a smarter way to do this. What are other professionals doing for early morning meetings on the Upper Hill route?

---

**Top community response — John M. (Verified Rider):**

The Eastlands–Upper Hill route via Jogoo Road and Haile Selassie has one specific problem: the Haile Selassie–Upper Hill junction between 7:15am and 8:30am. Everything coming into Upper Hill from the CBD side slows there.

**For a firm 8am meeting, here is the reverse schedule:**

- Arrive Upper Hill: 7:40am (20-minute buffer — this is non-negotiable for important meetings)
- Board Upper Hill-bound vehicle: 7:00am at latest
- Leave Umoja: 6:15am

I know 6:15am feels early. But here is the reframe: the importance of goal setting in your professional life includes protecting your first impressions. Arriving calm and 15 minutes early to a client meeting is a professional asset. Arriving breathless at 8:01am is a liability.

**The alternative nobody talks about:**

If you have any flexibility, propose that the 8am meeting moves to 8:30am. Many clients or managers will agree — and that 30 minutes changes everything about your morning journey and your mental state when you walk in.

**Response — Fatuma H.:**

Also: once you have a working departure time, write it down and treat it the same way you treat the meeting itself. Most professionals protect their meetings but leave their commute to chance. Your departure time is an appointment with your own performance.

This is one of the most underrated time management techniques in the Nairobi professional context — scheduling the commute, not just the destination.
