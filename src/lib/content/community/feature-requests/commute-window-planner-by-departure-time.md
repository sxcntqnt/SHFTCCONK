I want to put this clearly because I think a lot of people feel the same way.

I do not need a live map. I do not need to watch a matatu icon move. What I actually need is the answer to one question every morning:

**"Given what the community has reported this week, what is the best time for me to leave for my route today?"**

## What this would look like in practice

- I set my route: Kasarani → CBD
- I set my target arrival time: 8:15am
- Matatu Pulse shows me: based on community reports this week, leaving between 6:45am and 7:00am gives you the most consistent results for this arrival time

That is it. That is the product I would pay for.

## Why this matters more than tracking

Live tracking tells me what is happening right now. A departure window planner tells me what to do about it — before I have left home, while I still have choices.

The stress and stress management problem in Nairobi commuting is not that people do not know there is traffic. Everyone knows there is traffic. The problem is that they do not know **what to do about it before the day starts**.

A departure window planner built on community intelligence solves that. It turns Matatu Pulse from a tool you check in traffic to a tool you check the night before — which is where the real value is.
