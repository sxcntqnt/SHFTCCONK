Right now, the best intelligence on Matatu Pulse is buried in discussion threads. A first-time visitor to the Rongai category has no way to quickly find the most useful things regular commuters have learned.

## The request

On each category or route page, pin the top three community-verified tips — surfaced by upvotes and recency — at the top of the page before the discussions list.

**Example for the Rongai → CBD route:**

> **Tip 1:** Leave before 6:45am. After that, Bomas–Karen Hardy backs up and your journey expands by 40+ minutes.
>
> **Tip 2:** Board at Rongai terminus, not at Bomas or Hardy. You get a seat and a predictable departure.
>
> **Tip 3:** Fridays — leave before 3:30pm or after 8pm. The 4:30pm–7:30pm window is the worst traffic of the week on this route.

These three facts would save a new Rongai commuter weeks of painful learning.

## Why this matters for Brian

There is a version of Matatu Pulse that is a forum. And there is a version that is a commute intelligence tool. The pinned tips feature is what makes it the latter.

New professionals moving to Rongai, Kasarani, Syokimau, or Eastlands spend their first three months learning these patterns the hard way — arriving late, misjudging departure times, over-paying during peak fares.

Pinned, verified tips are how Matatu Pulse becomes the thing someone tells a friend about on their first week of work.
