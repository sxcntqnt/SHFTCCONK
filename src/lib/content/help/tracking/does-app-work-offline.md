---
title: "Does the app work when I have no data connection?"
excerpt: "Route maps and stage info are cached offline — live positions and ETAs need a data connection."
---

Core map tiles and route information are cached locally after your first visit, so you can view static route data offline.

Live vehicle positions and ETAs require a data connection — even EDGE (2G) is sufficient, since position updates are small payloads. On very poor connections you may notice positions updating every 30–60 seconds instead of in real time.

## Tips for low-connectivity areas

- Load your route before you leave an area with signal so the static map is cached
- The app automatically falls back to the last known position with a "stale" indicator when it can't reach the server
