---
title: "Why isn't my route showing any vehicles?"
excerpt: "GPS coverage depends on our sacco partner network — not all vehicles carry trackers yet."
---

This usually means no GPS-equipped vehicles are currently active on that route. Coverage depends on our sacco partner network — not all vehicles on every route carry trackers yet.

Check back during peak hours when more vehicles are typically active. You can request route coverage via the in-app form.

## Still not showing?

If a route has had zero vehicles for more than a day, [let us know](/help/tracking) — it may mean a sacco partner has paused their fleet.
