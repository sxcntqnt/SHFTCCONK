---
title: "Can I give my dispatcher access without giving them full account admin rights?"
excerpt: "Yes — invite them with the Dispatcher role under Settings → Team for scoped access."
---

Yes. The operator dashboard supports role-based access. Go to **Settings → Team** and invite your dispatcher with the **Dispatcher** role.

Dispatchers get access to the live fleet map and delay alerts, but not billing or account settings. This is the recommended setup for day-to-day fleet monitoring without exposing sensitive account details.

## Other available roles

- **Admin** — full access, including billing and team management
- **Dispatcher** — fleet map and delay alerts only
- **Analyst** — read-only access to analytics and exports, no live map control
