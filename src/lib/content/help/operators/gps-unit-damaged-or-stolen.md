---
title: "What happens if a GPS unit is damaged or stolen?"
excerpt: "Report it immediately so we can deactivate the unit — replacements ship within 2 business days."
---

Report lost or damaged hardware immediately to [ops@matatupulse.co.ke](mailto:ops@matatupulse.co.ke). We'll deactivate the unit to prevent phantom tracking data from appearing on your fleet map or in your analytics.

Replacement units are shipped within 2 business days for active partners. Hardware damage beyond normal wear is billed at cost — normal wear covers things like a cracked mounting bracket, not water damage or a unit that's been physically removed and lost.

## Preventing phantom data

A stolen or lost unit that's still powered will keep transmitting until deactivated, which is why reporting it right away matters — it keeps your fleet map accurate for your dispatchers in the meantime.
