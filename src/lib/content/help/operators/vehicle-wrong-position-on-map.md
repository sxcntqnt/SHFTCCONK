---
title: "My vehicle is showing the wrong position on the map. What do I do?"
excerpt: "Usually a GPS lock or power issue — check the unit's indicator light before filing a ticket."
---

GPS position errors are usually caused by poor satellite lock in dense urban areas or a GPS unit power issue.

First, check that the vehicle's GPS unit is powered and the indicator light is solid green. A blinking or amber light typically means the unit is still acquiring a satellite fix, which can take a few minutes after power-on, especially in areas with tall buildings.

If the issue persists beyond 30 minutes, submit a support ticket through your operator dashboard with the vehicle ID and a rough description of the vehicle's actual location at the time.

## Common causes

- Unit mounted with an obstructed view of the sky (under metal panelling, for example)
- Loose power connection causing intermittent resets
- Firmware needing an update — your dashboard will flag this automatically if detected
