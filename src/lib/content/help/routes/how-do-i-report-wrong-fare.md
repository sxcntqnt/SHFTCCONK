---
title: "How do I report a wrong fare or a route that's changed?"
excerpt: "Use the in-app 'Report an issue' button — corrections are reviewed within 24 hours."
---

Use the **Report an issue** button on any route or vehicle card in the app. Choose "Fare incorrect" or "Route changed" and add a short note — a stage name or fare amount is enough for our data team to investigate.

Your report is reviewed within 24 hours and corrections are applied directly to the platform, so other riders benefit from what you flagged.

## What counts as a route change

- A sacco has rerouted around roadworks or a closed road
- Stage locations have shifted
- Fares have changed for time-of-day or fuel-price reasons

Fare changes tied to fuel price adjustments are usually reflected automatically once a sacco partner updates their listing — reports mainly help us catch cases where that update hasn't happened yet.
