---
title: "How do I update my payment details?"
excerpt: "Settings → Billing → Payment Method. Changes apply from your next billing cycle."
---

Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Method** to update your card or M-Pesa details. Changes take effect from your next billing cycle — any in-progress charge on the old method will still complete.

If a payment fails, you'll get an email notification and a 5-day grace period before any service interruption, giving you time to update your details.

## Switching between card and M-Pesa

You can switch payment methods at any time. There's no fee for switching, and your billing history stays attached to your account regardless of which method you're on.
