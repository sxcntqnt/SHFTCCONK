---
title: "How do I download an invoice?"
excerpt: "Settings → Billing → Invoices. Each past invoice is available as a PDF."
---

Go to **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Every past invoice is listed with its date and amount, and can be downloaded as a PDF directly from there.

Invoices are generated automatically at the start of each billing cycle. If you need an invoice reissued with different company details (for example, an updated tax ID), contact [support@matatupulse.co.ke](mailto:support@matatupulse.co.ke) and we'll reissue it within one business day.

## Enterprise billing

Enterprise customers on annual contracts can request consolidated quarterly invoices instead of monthly ones — reach out to your account manager to set this up.
