---
title: "How do I create an account?"
excerpt: "Sign up with a phone number or email in under a minute — no verification wait for basic browsing."
---

Tap **Sign Up** on the app or at [matatupulse.co.ke/login](/login), then choose either:

- **Phone number** — you'll get a one-time SMS code to confirm
- **Email** — a confirmation link is sent immediately

You can browse live routes and vehicle positions without an account. An account is only required to save favourite routes, set up arrival alerts, or access an operator dashboard.

## Switching between rider and operator accounts

If you manage a fleet, you can request operator access from the same account under **Settings → Request Operator Access**. This is reviewed manually and typically approved within one business day once your sacco affiliation is confirmed.
