---
title: "Can I use Matatu Pulse without downloading the app?"
excerpt: "Yes — the full commuter experience is available in your mobile browser, no download required."
---

Yes. The full commuter experience — live map, ETA comparison, and route information — is available at [matatupulse.co.ke/app](/login) in your mobile browser. No download or account needed.

Push notifications for arrival alerts do require the app, since browsers can't reliably deliver background notifications on most Android and iOS devices.

## When you might still want the app

- You want arrival alerts pushed to your lock screen
- You're checking routes without a reliable data connection (the app caches route data locally)
- You want to save favourite routes and stages across sessions
