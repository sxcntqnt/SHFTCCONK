PART 5: APPENDIX

5.1 Glossary of Terms







Term



Definition





H3 Grid



Hexagonal hierarchical spatial index from Uber for analyzing urban movement patterns





Node Saturation



Metric measuring how many passengers pass through a transit point vs. its capacity





Transfer Velocity



Speed at which passengers move between transit connections





Walking-to-Waiting Ratio



Proportion of time commuters spend walking vs. waiting at transit nodes





Sacco



Savings and Credit Cooperative Organization — collective of matatu owners





Matatu



Kenyan minibus taxi, the backbone of Nairobi's informal transit





Node



Transit terminus or major interchange point





Terminus



End point of a matatu route





Live Pulse Map



Real-time GPS tracking interface for reserved fleet vehicles
